HEA-WORLD × REACHY MINI

Public beta RC · app 0.6.0

Give any public HEA a friendly physical presence.

Choose a publicly listed HEA, type a question, and hear its grounded answer through Reachy Mini Lite—with multilingual local speech, canonical emoji, and only physically allowlisted Pollen movements.

Release status: prepared for direct public RC publication. Clean catalog installation and owner physical validation are still required before stable release.

HOW IT WORKS

Four simple steps

  1. 1
    Connect Reachy

    Connect Reachy Mini Lite over USB and start it in Reachy Mini Control.

  2. 2
    Install and open

    After release, install this Python app from the Reachy application catalog and open its local control panel.

  3. 3
    Choose a public HEA

    Search the read-only HEA-World directory. Changing HEAs starts a fresh conversation.

  4. 4
    Type, hear, and see

    Reachy speaks each completed sentence in a matching installed Mac voice and adds safely gated expression.

REQUIREMENTS

Tested setup

  • Reachy Mini Lite connected over USB
  • Apple-silicon Mac and Reachy Mini Control 0.9.34
  • Python 3.12.14 and Reachy SDK/daemon 1.10.0
  • Internet for the public HEA directory and HEA answers
  • Installed macOS voices for multilingual offline speech

SAFETY

Motion stays local

  • Network data never becomes poses, joints, filenames, or motor commands.
  • Twenty-four emoji are visual; only four reviewed motion families can move.
  • Movement is capped at one per two sentences and recording sound stays off.
  • Stop & neutral cancels speech and motion, then attempts a neutral pose.

AI & PRIVACY

Know what leaves the Mac

Typed questions and the selected public HEA identity are sent to https://hea-world.com/api/reachymini/chat. The selected AI-powered HEA generates the answer and expression cues.

Speech is synthesized locally with macOS, played through Reachy's speaker, and deleted immediately. The app does not use the microphone or camera and stores no credential. A deliberate diagnostics export excludes conversation text, audio, visitor/session identifiers, and raw exceptions.

KNOWN BETA LIMITS

Current boundary

  • Only public directory-listed HEAs; no private, shared, draft, or unlisted access
  • No voice input, camera behavior, unattended operation, or wireless support claim
  • Only four official Pollen movement families have entered the supervised allowlist
  • Natural pronunciation depends on a matching installed macOS voice
